Countertop Shortlist

Warm-white, soft-veined options for the light-gray / navy-island / checkerboard kitchen. Tap an image to enlarge. Manufacturer photos — real slabs vary, especially quartzite.

Quartz: no sealing, no staining, but must always use a trivet (resin scorches ~300°F). Quartzite: tolerates heat, reseal ~yearly, every slab differs — pick yours in person and test it (glass scratch + lemon).
